**Vision Chat** is a private vision assistant that runs entirely on your
machine. Upload an image, ask about it, and keep the conversation going —
every answer takes the image and everything you've discussed into account.

### What you can do

- **Understand an image** — objects, colors, text, scenes, small details
- **Ask follow-ups** — the conversation remembers everything so far
- **Start fresh anytime** — a new chat is a new conversation about a new image

Upload an image and ask your first question to get started.
